## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

#### Zoom Account Information
When you authorize QuizTimer4Zoom through Zoom OAuth 2.0, we receive:
- Your Zoom user ID
- Your Zoom email address
- Your display name
- Your Zoom account status

**We do NOT request or store:**
- Your Zoom password
- Your contacts list
- Your call history
- Your profile picture
- Any other personal information

#### Session Information
We store the following during your session:
- Zoom authentication token (encrypted, session-only)
- Session ID
- Session creation timestamp
- Session expiration time (24 hours)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

#### Cookies and Similar Technology
We use session cookies to:
- Maintain your authenticated session
- Remember your session preferences
- Prevent cross-site attacks

**Cookie Details:**
- **Type:** Session cookie
- **Duration:** 24 hours
- **Purpose:** Authentication and session management
- **Encryption:** Secure (HTTPS only)
- **HttpOnly:** Yes (JavaScript cannot access)

### 2.3 Information from Zoom

Zoom provides us with:
- Your OAuth 2.0 authorization token
- Your Zoom user ID
- Your email address
- Your authorization scope and permissions

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

**Right of Access**
- Request what personal data we hold about you
- Receive data in machine-readable format

**Right to Rectification**
- Correct inaccurate data
- Complete incomplete data

**Right to Erasure**
- Request deletion of your data
- Right to be "forgotten" (with exceptions)

**Right to Restrict Processing**
- Limit how we use your data
- Prevent processing while reviewing

**Right to Data Portability**
- Export your data in standard format
- Transfer to another provider

**Right to Object**
- Object to processing in certain circumstances
- Opt-out of marketing communications

**Right to Lodge Complaint**
- File complaint with data protection authority
- No retaliation for complaints

### 7.2 CCPA Rights (California Users)

**Right to Know**
- Request what personal information we collect
- How it's used
- Who it's shared with

**Right to Delete**
- Request deletion of personal information
- Exceptions for legal compliance

**Right to Opt-Out**
- Opt-out of data sales (we don't sell data)
- Opt-out of targeted advertising (we don't do this)

**Right to Non-Discrimination**
- No discrimination for exercising privacy rights
- No denial of service or higher prices

### 7.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

**Submit Requests To:**
- Email: [privacy@yourdomain.com]
- Response time: 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA)
- Verification: May require identity verification

**Request Types:**
- Access Request
- Deletion Request
- Portability Request
