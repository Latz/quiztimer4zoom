Privacy Policy for Quiztimer4Zoom
Last Updated: October 21, 2025

Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how Quiztimer4Zoom ("we," "our," or "the App") handles information when you use our application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect
Quiztimer4Zoom collects and stores only basic application configuration data necessary for the App to function properly. This may include:

Application settings and preferences
User interface customization options
Timer configurations
Display preferences
What We Don't Collect
We want to be clear about what we do NOT collect:

Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
User account data
Usage analytics or tracking data
Location data
Device information beyond what is necessary for app functionality
Any content from your Zoom meetings or sessions
How We Use Information
The configuration data we store is used solely to:

Maintain your app settings between sessions
Ensure the App functions according to your preferences
Provide a consistent user experience
Data Storage
All configuration data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, share, or store your data on external servers or cloud services.

Data Sharing
We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any data with third parties. Since we only store local configuration data, there is no information to share with anyone.

Third-Party Services
Quiztimer4Zoom operates in conjunction with Zoom but does not integrate with Zoom's data or access any meeting content. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Security
Your configuration data is stored securely on your local device. We implement reasonable security measures to protect the integrity of the stored configuration data.

Data Retention and Deletion
Configuration data remains on your device until you:

Uninstall the App
Clear the App's data through your device settings
Reset the App to default settings

Children's Privacy
Quiztimer4Zoom does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Your Rights
You have complete control over your configuration data. You can:

Reset all settings to defaults at any time
Delete all stored data by uninstalling the App
Modify any configuration settings within the App
