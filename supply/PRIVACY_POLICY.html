1 Privacy Policy

Fuzzy Monster - QuizTimer4Zoom Application Effective Date: 2025-10-28 Last Updated: 2025-10-28 Company: Fuzzy Monster Domain: fuzzy.monster

1.1 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how QuizTimer4Zoom (the “App”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our Zoom application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Application.

1.2 2. Information We Collect

1.2.1 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

1.2.1.1 Zoom Account Information

When you authorize QuizTimer4Zoom through Zoom OAuth 2.0, we receive: - Your Zoom user ID - Your Zoom email address - Your display name - Your Zoom account status

We do NOT request or store: - Your Zoom password - Your contacts list - Your call history - Your profile picture - Any other personal information

1.2.1.2 Session Information

We store the following during your session: - Zoom authentication token (encrypted, session-only) - Session ID - Session creation timestamp - Session expiration time (24 hours)

1.2.2 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

1.2.2.1 Cookies and Similar Technology

We use session cookies to: - Maintain your authenticated session - Remember your session preferences - Prevent cross-site attacks

Cookie Details: - Type: Session cookie - Duration: 24 hours - Purpose: Authentication and session management - Encryption: Secure (HTTPS only) - HttpOnly: Yes (JavaScript cannot access)

1.2.3 2.3 Information from Zoom

Zoom provides us with: - Your OAuth 2.0 authorization token - Your Zoom user ID - Your email address - Your authorization scope and permissions

1.3 3. How We Use Your Information

1.3.1 3.1 Primary Uses

Authentication & Authorization - Verify your identity through Zoom OAuth - Maintain secure authenticated sessions - Prevent unauthorized access

Application Functionality - Enable you to use QuizTimer4Zoom features within Zoom - Customize your experience - Provide technical support

Security & Safety - Detect and prevent fraud - Protect against malicious activity - Secure our systems and users - Enforce our Terms of Service

1.3.2 3.2 What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not share your data with advertisers
  • We do not use your data for marketing purposes
  • We do not combine your data with other services
  • We do not retain data longer than necessary

1.4 4. Data Retention

1.4.1 4.1 Session Data

Duration: 24 hours or until logout Storage: Server memory/session store only Deletion: Automatic after expiration

1.4.2 4.2 Authentication Tokens

Duration: While session is active Storage: Encrypted session storage (not persistent) Deletion: Automatic on logout or expiration

1.4.3 4.3 Access Logs

Duration: 30 days Retention Purpose: Security monitoring and troubleshooting Deletion: Automatic purge after 30 days

1.4.4 4.4 Error/Crash Logs

Duration: 30 days Retention Purpose: Debugging and improvement Deletion: Automatic purge after 30 days

1.4.5 4.5 Security Incident Records

Duration: 1 year Retention Purpose: Legal compliance and investigation Deletion: Automatic purge after 1 year

1.4.6 4.6 Backup Data

Duration: Up to 30 days Purpose: System recovery and disaster prevention Deletion: Automatic purge after backup period

1.5 5. Information Sharing

1.5.1 5.1 Who We Share Data With

With Zoom - Your authentication is validated through Zoom OAuth - Minimal information shared necessary for authentication - Zoom’s privacy policy applies to Zoom’s processing

1.5.2 6.2 Third-Party Services

Third-Party APIs Used: - Zoom OAuth 2.0 - Authentication service - Handles all password security - Provides user identification - Privacy: https://zoom.us/privacy

Zoom’s Privacy Policy - Data shared with Zoom is governed by Zoom’s privacy policy - You can review: https://zoom.us/privacy

1.5.3 6.3 International Transfers

Data Location: - Application data stored in EU - Subject to laws of jurisdiction where stored - GDPR/CCPA compliant transfers

1.6 7. Your Privacy Rights

1.6.1 7.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

Right of Access - Request what personal data we hold about you - Receive data in machine-readable format

Right to Rectification - Correct inaccurate data - Complete incomplete data

Right to Erasure - Request deletion of your data - Right to be “forgotten” (with exceptions)

Right to Restrict Processing - Limit how we use your data - Prevent processing while reviewing

Right to Data Portability - Export your data in standard format - Transfer to another provider

Right to Object - Object to processing in certain circumstances - Opt-out of marketing communications

Right to Lodge Complaint - File complaint with data protection authority - No retaliation for complaints

1.6.2 7.2 CCPA Rights (California Users)

Right to Know - Request what personal information we collect - How it’s used - Who it’s shared with

Right to Delete - Request deletion of personal information - Exceptions for legal compliance

Right to Opt-Out - Opt-out of data sales (we don’t sell data) - Opt-out of targeted advertising (we don’t do this)

Right to Non-Discrimination - No discrimination for exercising privacy rights - No denial of service or higher prices

1.6.3 7.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

Submit Requests To: - Email: [privacy@yourdomain.com] - Response time: 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA) - Verification: May require identity verification

Request Types: - Access Request - Deletion Request - Portability Request - Correction Request - Objection Request

1.7 8. Data Minimization

1.7.1 8.1 What We Don’t Collect

We do NOT collect: - ✗ Passwords (handled by Zoom) - ✗ Payment information - ✗ Credit card data - ✗ Biometric data - ✗ Health information - ✗ Precise GPS location - ✗ Call recordings - ✗ Screen recordings - ✗ Keystroke logging - ✗ Social media handles - ✗ Family relationships - ✗ Political beliefs - ✗ Religious affiliation

1.7.2 8.2 Minimal Data Principle

We follow the principle of data minimization: - Only collect data we need - Only use data as necessary - Only retain data as long as needed - Automatically delete data when possible

1.8 9. Children’s Privacy

1.8.1 9.1 Age Restrictions

QuizTimer4Zoom is not intended for children under 13 (COPPA) or 16 (GDPR).

We do not knowingly collect information from children: - If we discover we have collected data from a child - We will delete it immediately - Contact us if you believe we have such data

Parental Controls: - Zoom’s parental controls apply - We comply with all children’s privacy laws

1.9.2 10.2 Do Not Track

We respect “Do Not Track” signals where feasible: - We don’t use tracking technology - Third-party tracking is minimal - Zoom’s own tracking is outside our control

1.15 16. Frequently Asked Questions

1.15.1 Q: Do you sell my data?

A: No. We do not sell any personal data to anyone, period.

1.15.2 Q: How long do you keep my data?

A: Session data: 24 hours. Logs: 30 days. Never longer than necessary.

1.15.3 Q: Can I request my data?

A: Yes. Email info@fuzzy.monster for a data access request.

1.15.4 Q: Can you delete my data?

A: Yes. We’ll delete all data upon request (some legal records may be retained).

1.15.5 Q: Is my data encrypted?

A: Yes. All data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest is encrypted.

1.15.6 Q: Who can access my data?

A: Only authorized personnel with a legitimate business need.

1.15.7 Q: What about my Zoom data?

A: We don’t access your Zoom account beyond authentication. Zoom’s privacy policy governs that.

1.15.8 Q: What if there’s a data breach?

A: We will notify you within 72 hours as required by law.

1.15.9 Q: Can I use this app without giving all permissions?

A: Only the minimum required for authentication cannot be avoided.
